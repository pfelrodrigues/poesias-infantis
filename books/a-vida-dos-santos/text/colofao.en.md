# Colophon

A handwritten notebook by Maria Luiza, Talita's grandmother, written from memory. This is a free reading edition. It is not for sale.

The original text remains in the notebook. This edition revises spelling and punctuation, checks feast days and life dates, and orders the pieces by the calendar, from January to December. Prayers stay with their saint.

The 61 original pieces are **collated**. They have not yet gone through family **proofed** review. This editorial colophon remains a **draft**.

The page-by-page diplomatic transcription lives in the working transcription repository. The scans are not in this repository.

The saints' lives themselves remain in Portuguese.

The cover of this edition is a painting made for the notebook. Saint images come from Wikimedia Commons, in the public domain or under a Creative Commons license. Author and license for each file are listed in `images/credits.json`.
